### Opportunity 1: TenantEase Multimodal Maintenance & Lease Intelligence Agent
*Vertical: PropTech / Real Estate Operations (TenantEase SaaS)*

* **The Problem:**
  Property managers using TenantEase manage thousands of rental units. When a tenant encounters a maintenance issue (e.g., a burst pipe, HVAC failure, or electrical short), the intake process is chaotic. Tenants submit vague descriptions or unformatted photos. Property managers must manually:
  1. Inspect the ticket to gauge emergency severity.
  2. Search through multi-page lease PDFs to verify whether the landlord or tenant is contractually liable for the repair under local tenancy law.
  3. Contact vetted contractors for scheduling.
  4. Manually update tenants via WhatsApp or SMS.
  This takes 4 to 24 hours per ticket, increases vacancy churn, and risks costly structural water or fire damage.

* **The AI/ML Solution:**
  An autonomous, multimodal maintenance agent integrated directly into TenantEase:
  - **Multimodal Visual Inspection:** Ingests tenant-submitted photos and voice notes via Gemini 2.0 Flash Multimodal, classifying issue severity (1-5 scale) and detecting hazard markers.
  - **Contractual Lease Clause RAG:** Performs hybrid vector retrieval (pgvector + BM25) over the tenant's signed lease agreement, pinpointing the exact clause governing appliance/plumbing maintenance liability.
  - **Autonomous Dispatch:** If liability falls on the landlord and severity is high, the agent automatically issues a work order to preferred contractors and sends structured WhatsApp status updates.

* **Strategic & Financial Impact:**
  - 75% reduction in ticket triage and resolution cycle time.
  - 40% reduction in property manager operational overhead.
  - Provides TenantEase with an unfair competitive moat against legacy property software (Buildium, AppFolio).

### Opportunity 2: Revio Autonomous Sentiment-to-Action & Reputation Engine
*Vertical: Customer Reputation & Brand Growth (Revio SaaS)*

* **The Problem:**
  Multi-location brands, restaurants, clinics, and SaaS providers on Revio receive hundreds of public customer reviews weekly across Google Maps, Trustpilot, and app stores. Negative reviews linger unaddressed for days, driving down local SEO rankings and escalating customer churn. Conversely, generic canned responses ("Thank you for your feedback!") alienate customers and harm brand reputation.

* **The AI/ML Solution:**
  An autonomous sentiment-to-action engine embedded in Revio:
  - **Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA):** Deconstructs unstructured reviews into granular aspect scores (Staff Friendliness, Speed of Service, Pricing, Cleanliness, Product Reliability).
  - **Crisis & Brand Safety Detection:** Flags critical escalation signals (legal threats, food poisoning, discrimination, severe billing bugs) and triggers emergency Slack/SMS webhooks to executives.
  - **Persona-Aligned Voice Synthesizer:** Drafts hyper-contextual, empathetic responses matching the client's custom brand voice guidelines. Supports auto-publishing for high-confidence positive reviews and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) approval queues for negative reviews.

* **Strategic & Financial Impact:**
  - Response turnaround drops from 48 hours to under 3 minutes.
  - 3x increase in review response rate, directly lifting Google Maps local search visibility for Revio clients.
  - Transforms Revio from a passive dashboard into an active, autonomous customer experience platform.
